# Day 07 — Claude Model Selection & Effort Settings

> **#60DaysClaudeChallenge** | Day 7 of 60

---

## 🧠 What I Learned Today

Most people open Claude and just start typing — same model, same settings, every single time.

Today I learned that's like driving everywhere in first gear.

The real unlock? **Match the model to the task.**

---

## ⚡ The Three Claude Models

### Haiku — Speed Lane
For quick, lightweight tasks where speed matters more than depth.
- Grammar & spell checks
- Simple definitions
- Flashcard generation
- Rephrasing sentences

### Sonnet — Daily Driver ★
Your go-to for 80%+ of everyday work. Smart, fast, balanced.
- Coding & debugging
- Research summaries
- Essay & cover letter writing
- Interview prep & learning

### Opus — The Big Gun
Reserve for high-stakes, complex tasks only.
- Final resume polish
- Complex algorithms & system design
- Deep research synthesis
- End-semester reports

---

## ⚙️ Effort Settings

| Level | When to use |
|-------|-------------|
| Low | Fast answers, simple rewrites |
| **Standard** ★ | Your everyday default |
| High | When quality really counts |
| Max | Final deliverables only |

---

## 🗺️ My Task → Model Map

| Task | Model | Effort |
|------|-------|--------|
| Debugging code | Sonnet | Standard |
| Complex algorithms | Opus | High |
| Resume (draft) | Sonnet | High |
| Resume (final) | Opus | Max |
| Quick definitions | Haiku | Low |
| Research summary | Sonnet | High |
| Interview prep | Sonnet | Standard |
| Grammar check | Haiku | Low |

---

## 🚫 Mistakes I'll Stop Making

1. Using Opus for everything — overkill kills speed
2. Writing vague prompts — specificity = better output
3. Not iterating — the first response is a draft, not the final answer
4. Max effort on routine tasks — wasteful and slower
5. Trusting code without testing — Claude is a pair programmer, not a compiler

---

## ✅ Key Takeaway

> If I could use only ONE model and ONE setting for most of my work:
>
> **Claude Sonnet + Standard effort.**
>
> It handles coding, research, essays, and career prep — all with excellent quality and speed. Upgrade to Opus + Max only when the stakes are genuinely high.
