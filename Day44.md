## Key Learnings

1. **The skills section is the most underrated field on a profile.** Tools you actually use in your project descriptions (Pandas, Tableau) can be completely absent from your skills list simply because it's the field people fill out once and never revisit. LinkedIn search weights it heavily, so this quiet gap is often the single biggest reason a strong profile stays unfound.
2. **A great hook doesn't guarantee a great About section.** The opening two lines can score well on charisma alone, but the sections after it need actual numbers, not just activity descriptions, or the strength of the hook gets wasted.
3. **An empty experience entry is worse than a short one.** A blank role next to a strong one doesn't read as "still writing this up," it reads as "didn't do much here" — even when the opposite is true.
4. **Your best proof point is often buried in the wrong section.** A result as strong as an R² of 0.998 was sitting in the experience section, invisible to anyone who reads only the About section and moves on.
5. **Fixing wording matters less than fixing placement.** Most of the actual rewriting here was moving existing facts to where they'd be seen and pairing them with numbers, not inventing new content.
